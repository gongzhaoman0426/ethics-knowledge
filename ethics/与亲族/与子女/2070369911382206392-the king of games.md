#the king of games#不知道你们意识到没有，“过家家”是游戏之王。它可以临时捏造任何剧情，把任意年龄差的小孩粘在一起，创造出无穷的角色，让每一个孩子都加入，剧情还可以无限延续。没有相机、没有导演也没有观众的舞台上，用最敷衍的道具，用着最大的热情，演着最无意的剧情。乐此不疲。They are purely living in the moment, free from all the gravity of reality.No worries and no ambitions, just experiencing the drama of all those lives.They love everything, embrace all fates.They die the most horrible deaths and giggle afterwards.They don’t discriminate or hate,switching from the wealthiest to the poorest in the blink of an eye, as if it’s nothing.Like the Buddha, having their 72 reincarnations on a Thursday afternoon.The game itself is a form of art, a miraculous metaphor for humankind.The life we chased for our whole lives was given to us at the very beginning.We had it,and we lost it,and we forgot about it.And we went on searching for it.This is the king of games.Let the kids have their fair share of itbefore it’s too late.
