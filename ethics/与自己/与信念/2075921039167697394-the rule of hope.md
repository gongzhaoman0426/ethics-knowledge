#the rule of hope#You can hope as much as you want, my love,as long as you can bear the weight when it ain’t.And remember, at the end of the day,you, and you alone, are the sole bearer of that stance.I’m willing to help, I really am,but to be frank—I can’t.I really can't.That's the rule of hope,from the beginning of our kindto the end of time.
